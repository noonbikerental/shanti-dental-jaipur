Many patients put off visiting a dentist when a cavity starts hurting because they fear that a Root Canal Treatment (RCT) will be painful. In reality, **an infected tooth causes pain—root canal treatment removes that pain** and lets you keep your natural tooth.

At Shanti Dental Hospital in Kishanpole Bazar, we explain every step calmly before starting so you feel comfortable from the very first minute.

## When Does a Tooth Need a Root Canal?

When tooth decay goes deeper than the hard outer enamel and reaches the soft nerve (pulp) inside, simple fillings are no longer enough. Common warning signs include:

- Sharp or lingering pain when drinking hot tea or cold water
- A throbbing toothache that worsens when lying down at night
- Pain when biting or chewing food on one side
- Swelling or a small pimple-like bump on the gums near a tooth

If you notice these symptoms early, saving the tooth is simpler and more predictable.

## How Painless Root Canal Treatment Works

Modern endodontic instruments and effective local anaesthesia make root canal therapy smooth and quick. Here is what typically happens during your visit:

1. **Digital X-Ray & Clear Diagnosis**: We take a quick X-ray to see the exact shape of the roots and extent of infection, then explain whether the tooth needs an RCT or a simple filling.
2. **Gentle Local Anaesthesia**: The area around the tooth is numbed thoroughly before the procedure begins. Most patients feel only mild pressure, not sharp pain.
3. **Cleaning & Shaping the Canals**: Using fine rotary instruments, the infected nerve tissue is gently removed and the inside of the tooth is disinfected.
4. **Sealing the Tooth**: Once clean and dry, the canals are filled with a biocompatible material so bacteria cannot enter again.
5. **Protective Dental Cap (Crown)**: For back chewing teeth, placing a strong tooth-coloured crown protects the treated tooth from cracking under normal biting forces.

## How Long Does It Take?

Depending on how settled the infection is, many straightforward root canals can be completed comfortably in **1 to 2 appointments** of 20 to 30 minutes each.

> **Tip from Dr. Shubham Gupta:** Do not wait until swelling appears or painkillers stop working. Early treatment saves time, reduces appointments, and preserves more natural tooth structure.

## Caring for Your Tooth After RCT

For a few days after your appointment, it is normal for the tooth to feel slightly tender when biting. Stick to softer foods on that side until your final crown is placed, keep brushing twice daily, and avoid biting hard nuts or ice on an uncapped tooth.
